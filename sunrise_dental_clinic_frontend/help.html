SUNRISE DENTAL CLINIC - STAFF HELP GUIDE
=========================================

1. LOGIN
   Enter your username and password, then click Login.
   Receptionist accounts can register/browse/search/bill appointments and
   look up patients. Admin accounts can additionally manage dentists,
   treatments, staff accounts and view reports.

2. REGISTER APPOINTMENT
   Click 'Register Appointment'. If the patient has visited before, pick them
   from the 'Existing Patient' dropdown to auto-fill and lock their details
   (this avoids creating a duplicate patient record). For a first-time
   patient, leave it on '-- New Patient --' and fill in their name, address
   and contact number yourself. Choose a dentist and treatment, pick the date
   and time from the pickers, then submit. The appointment number is
   generated automatically.

3. BROWSE APPOINTMENTS
   Click 'Browse Appointments' to see and filter appointments by date, date
   range, dentist or patient, instead of looking one up by exact number.
   Each row has Update, Cancel and Bill actions.

4. SEARCH / MANAGE APPOINTMENT
   Click 'Search / Manage Appointment' and enter the appointment number to view
   its details. From there you can Update the schedule, Cancel the appointment,
   or Calculate & Print its Bill.

5. CALCULATE & PRINT BILL
   From the search or browse screen, click 'Calculate & Print Bill' (or Bill).
   The total is the fixed consultation fee plus the chosen treatment's fee.
   Use Print to send it to a printer, or Close to dismiss the receipt.

6. MANAGE PATIENTS
   Click 'Manage Patients' and search by name or contact number. Click a
   result row to see that patient's full appointment history.

7. ADMIN: MANAGE DENTISTS / TREATMENTS
   Add a new dentist/treatment using the form fields and the Add button, or select
   an existing row in the table and click Update Selected to edit it.

8. ADMIN: REPORTS
   Choose a report (appointments per day, revenue per dentist, or upcoming
   appointments) and click Generate.

9. ADMIN: MANAGE STAFF ACCOUNTS
   Click 'Manage Staff Accounts' to create receptionist or admin logins, or
   to deactivate/reactivate an existing one. A deactivated account is signed
   out immediately and can no longer log in. You cannot deactivate your own
   account.

10. EXIT
   Click Logout on the main menu and confirm to return to the login screen.
