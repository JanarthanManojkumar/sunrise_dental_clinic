SUNRISE DENTAL CLINIC - STAFF HELP GUIDE
=========================================

1. LOGIN
   Enter your username and password, then click Login.
   Receptionist accounts can register/search/bill appointments.
   Admin accounts can additionally manage dentists, treatments and view reports.

2. REGISTER APPOINTMENT
   Click 'Register Appointment', fill in the patient's name, address and contact
   number, choose a dentist and treatment, then enter the date (YYYY-MM-DD) and
   time (HH:mm). The appointment number is generated automatically.

3. SEARCH / MANAGE APPOINTMENT
   Click 'Search / Manage Appointment' and enter the appointment number to view
   its details. From there you can Update the schedule, Cancel the appointment,
   or Calculate & Print its Bill.

4. CALCULATE & PRINT BILL
   From the search screen, click 'Calculate & Print Bill'. The total is the fixed
   consultation fee plus the chosen treatment's fee. Use Print to send it to a
   printer, or Close to dismiss the receipt.

5. ADMIN: MANAGE DENTISTS / TREATMENTS
   Add a new dentist/treatment using the form fields and the Add button, or select
   an existing row in the table and click Update Selected to edit it.

6. ADMIN: REPORTS
   Choose a report (appointments per day, revenue per dentist, or upcoming
   appointments) and click Generate.

7. EXIT
   Click Logout on the main menu and confirm to return to the login screen.
